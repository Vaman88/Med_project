# Cooking game visual references

Study material only, collected 2026-11-04 from each game's official store page or press kit.
None of these images ship in the app. Healthy Steps draws its own original SVG food art.

| File | Game | Source | What it informs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cooking-mama-01.jpg` | Cooking Mama: Cuisine! | [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cooking-mama-cuisine/id1563901930) (screenshot 1) | Pan cooking framed tightly around the stove, with the timer and tool kept beside the food. |
| `cooking-mama-02.jpg` | Cooking Mama: Cuisine! | same listing (screenshot 2) | Close-up cutting: the food fills the board, and each cut visibly separates a slice that falls away from the knife. This is the model for our cut step. |
| `cooking-mama-03.jpg` | Cooking Mama: Cuisine! | same listing (screenshot 3) | Ingredient overview: each ingredient sits in its own small container, readable at a glance. |
| `venba-2.jpg` | Venba | [Press kit](https://venbagame.com/press/), image 2 | Overhead cutting board with the knife off to the side, a flat warm background and strong food silhouettes. |
| `venba-4.jpg` | Venba | press kit, image 4 | Overhead workspace: bowls of prepared ingredients ring the main pot, which stays the focal point. |
| `venba-6.jpg` | Venba | press kit, image 6 | Tools and containers placed around one central cooking surface, with calm, warm colors. |
| `venba-7.jpg` | Venba | press kit, image 7 | Finished dish filling the frame, with visible texture on every piece. This sets the bar for our finish screen. |
| `venba-8.jpg` | Venba | press kit, image 8 | Plated food in a tray, garnish details, and soft contact shadows. |
| `food-stylist-screenshots.png` | Food Stylist | [foodstylistgame.com](https://foodstylistgame.com/) (store screenshot strip) | Plating and arranging on a phone in portrait: a big plate on top with an ingredient tray below. |
| `good-pizza-01.jpg` | Good Pizza, Great Pizza | [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id911121200), screenshot 1 | Focused assembly: one dish in the middle, ingredient bins in a row at the top edge. |
| `good-pizza-02.jpg` | Good Pizza, Great Pizza | same listing, screenshot 3 | Toppings scattered by the player stay exactly where they were placed. |
