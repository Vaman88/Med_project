## Photos from the ChatGPT/Codex set (Pexels licence)

`public/images/child/`, `public/images/adult/` and `public/images/meals/` hold Pexels photographs (free to use, attribution not required but credited). Photographers and links are in `docs/child-photo-credits.md`, `docs/adult-photo-credits.md` and `docs/expanded-meal-photo-credits.md`. The kids' menu uses `child/*` for the six original dishes and `meals/{avocado,fruit,oats}.jpg` for avocado toast, fruit salad and oatmeal. The adult planner uses `adult/<meal-id>.jpg` first, then a keyword-matched `meals/*.jpg`, then the older images. `adult/bean-rice.jpg` was left out because the photo shows minced meat.
