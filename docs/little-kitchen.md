# Little Kitchen

The home page offers **Parent sign-up / Set up a child’s kitchen**. A four-step wizard creates a device-local child profile with nickname and avatar, pantry, allergies, dietary choices, equipment, household details, budget, favorites, and a Parent PIN. It does not create a Supabase login or send child information to a server. The PIN gates settings within the app, but browser storage is not encrypted; it is not a security boundary against someone with access to this device's site data.

- Ages **5–12** enter Little Kitchen. **13–17** retain the existing meal planner.
- The parent pantry uses type-to-search suggestions from 98 common foods and accepts any additional food name. Selected foods appear as removable chips. Six pantry-dependent recipes are playable: black bean/oat/cocoa brownies, vegetable pita pizzas, banana oat pancakes, banana berry yogurt parfait, rainbow veggie dippers, and a canned bean and corn bowl. Missing ingredients are shown before play; a custom food does not create an unreviewed recipe automatically.
- Selected ingredient allergies hide conflicting recipes. An unrecognized exclusion pauses suggestions. Package labels and cross-contact still require caregiver review.
- The child menu has Cook, My Recipes, and My Chef destinations, recipe art, category filters, pantry readiness, and earned badges. Completed recipes and stars persist in this browser and can be replayed from the cookbook.
- Each recipe opens one viewport-height kitchen counter with an ingredient shelf, cutting board, bowl, prep cup, baking tray or skillet, oven or stove, serving plate, and four selectable tools. Ingredients and tools can be dragged with a pointer or selected and placed with taps or keyboard activation. On mobile, station tabs move between the same work zones without vertical page scrolling.
- Four banana or pepper pieces change appearance after each of three presses or cuts. A progress bar tracks preparation. Brownies and pancakes require ingredient transfers, eight whisk strokes, pouring, and cooking. Pizza adds a sauce-spreading action and visible toppings at drop locations. Oven recipes require closing the door, turning the dial, baking or skipping the wait, opening the door, and serving. Pancakes require cooking, three spatula flips, cooking the other side, and serving. A task card guides each action while the counter stays visible.
- The three no-cook recipes have a separate counter flow with a three-tap prep board, selectable ingredients, a bowl or serving plate, a Chef Challenge, and serving. Each is playable with touch, mouse, or keyboard and saves to the same cookbook.
- The cooking layout grows with its instructions and controls, including on short phone screens. The original counter recipes have a one-tap placement action after a food is selected, and all ingredient labels remain readable. The work zones and tool rack can be reached by page scrolling.
- Each game has two short ingredient-linked Chef Challenges with gentle retries, a pause control, sound-off default, read-aloud step support when speech synthesis is available, and reduced-motion styling. Completion shows the finished dish, stars, a fact, cookbook saving, and a grown-up recipe sheet.
- The supplied **Little Kitchen – Healthy Steps** HTML pizza game is incorporated into the platform's Rainbow mini pizzas recipe. Its prep, sauce spreading, topping placement, pretend oven, serving, pause, sound, and quiz flow uses the existing child profile and cookbook. Three chopped pepper pieces can be placed at different pizza positions; the Prep / Make / Cook / Serve tracker shows the current stage. The game's independent local storage and allergy toggles are replaced by the platform's parent-managed pantry and food settings.
- The six-second cooking timer is explicitly pretend. Real cooking measurements, instructions, tools, warnings, supervision guidance, and affordable substitution notes are in the parent-facing recipe sheet. Exact cost estimates are intentionally omitted because no verified store price is available for these recipes.
- Settings can be edited or deleted after PIN entry. Deleting the child profile also clears child progress. Child planner data uses a separate key from the original guest pantry. Parent settings and game progress persist in this browser, with feedback when browser saving fails.
- The menu currently contains six playable recipes. A larger recipe library, a parent shopping list, more avatar clothing and hat styles, and account-backed sync remain future work. An unknown allergy or exclusion pauses recommendations, and real labels and cross-contact still require caregiver review.
